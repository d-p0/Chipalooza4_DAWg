# List of test equipment
Available Test Equipments

--- 
### Table of test equipment
| Equipment | Intended use |
|---|---|
|Digital generator | Apply digital input codes: 12 control bits (10 tuning + 2 divider), plus enable, reference and spare lines (up to 16 digital lines in total)|
|Digital oscilloscope (Keysight EDUX1002G)| Observe CLK_OUT: frequency, duty cycle, edge times, jitter, start/stop behaviour and settling time after a tuning change |
|Function generator| Generate a reference clock for REF_IN (if the reference mode is included), and other test signals if required |
|DC power supply| Power the chip and test board, and sweep the supply voltage (1.62 to 1.95 V) to measure frequency and supply current against supply |
|Digital multimeter| Measure DC voltages on the internal bias/control node (through the analogue mux), and supply current |
|LCR meter| Check the values of passive components (for example decoupling capacitors) used on the test board |
|Soldering station| Assemble and modify the test board and breakout boards |
|PCB prototyping equipment| Build custom test boards for the chip |
|PCB test boards / breakout boards| Connect the packaged chip to the equipment above |
